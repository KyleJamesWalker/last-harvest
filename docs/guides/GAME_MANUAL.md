# Last Harvest manual

Last Harvest is a zombie survivors-like for the ANBERNIC RG DS Plus. It also plays in a browser, on Android, on muOS handhelds and on desktops. You hold a farm through one night while your weapons fire on their own, then run home at dawn. This manual covers playing first, then making your own zombies, looks, props, maps and mod packs in the card editor.

## Controls

Your weapons fire on their own. You move, turn, throw grenades and pick upgrades.

### Buttons and keys

| Action | RG DS | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Walk forward and back | Up and Down, or the stick | Up and Down arrows |
| Strafe | Left and Right, or the stick | Left and Right arrows |
| Turn | Y and A | A and X |
| Turn around | B | Z |
| Aim at barrels | Hold X | Hold S |
| Throw a grenade | Tap the map | Click the bottom screen |
| Throw a grenade straight ahead | Hold R1, then let go | Hold W, then let go |
| Pick an upgrade | Tap it, or Up, Down and X | Click it, or the arrows and S |
| Pause | Start | Enter, or Esc |
| Back out of a menu | B | Z, or Esc |
| Back to the title | Back, or B after a run | Tab, or Z after a run |
| Screenshot | R2 | 2 |
| Frame-rate overlay | Select | Backspace |
| Volume | V+ and V- | `=` and `-` |
| Quit | Hold Home for one second | Cmd+Q, or close the window |

R1 is the quick throw. With a grenade ready, R1 puts a marker on the ground 4 tiles ahead. Holding R1 slides the marker out to 8 tiles over one second, and letting go throws there. Nothing slows down, and the marker follows you as you walk and turn. With no grenade ready, R1 clicks.

On the RG DS, closing the lid pauses the game and turns off both screens. After 5 minutes closed, the device sleeps. The power button also puts it to sleep, and the game resumes where it left off. Plugging in headphones moves the sound to them.

### One screen

With Settings > Screens set to One, a run plays on the top screen alone and the bottom screen goes dark.

| Action | RG DS | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Show the map | Hold L1 | Hold 1 |
| Aim a grenade, in slowed time | Hold L2, then the d-pad or stick | Hold Q, then the arrows |
| Throw it | Let go of L2 | Let go of Q |
| Cancel the throw | B | Z |
| Throw a grenade straight ahead | Hold R1, then let go | Hold W, then let go |

- Your grenade count shows only on the map. A grenade on your belt means one is ready.
- With no grenade ready, L2 shows the map at full speed and clicks.
- While you aim, you keep walking in the direction you held. After 3 seconds, the grenade throws itself.
- The cursor starts where your last throw went if that was under 5 seconds ago, or else straight ahead.

### Touch

On a phone, in a browser window, and in a desktop window, a run plays on one screen with controls beside it. When the screen is upright, the controls sit under it.

| Action | Touch |
|---|---|
| Walk and strafe | Put a thumb down left of the picture and drag. The stick follows it. |
| Turn | Slide a thumb sideways on the strip under Around. Further turns faster. |
| Turn around | Around |
| Show the map | Grenade. Tap it again to put the 3D view back. You keep moving. |
| Throw a grenade | While the map is up, tap where it should land |
| Pick an upgrade, or a pause choice | Tap it |
| Pause | Pause |

Two settings change the touch controls:

- **Grenades:** Touch, the default, works as above. Pad makes Grenade, and L2 on a gamepad, aim a cursor in slowed time, as on one screen.
- **Touch controls:** Auto, the default, hides the controls when you use a gamepad or keyboard and shows them at your next touch. On keeps them up. Off removes them, for a touch screen with buttons. Until you press a button, a touch turns Off back to Auto.

### Gamepads

A gamepad maps by its labels, as on an Xbox pad:

- A, B, X and Y are the same buttons.
- The bumpers and triggers are L1, R1, L2 and R2.
- View is Select, and Menu is Start.
- The d-pad and left stick move you, and the right stick turns.

## The night

A night lasts ten minutes, from dusk to dawn. A bell rings each minute.

- **Zombies rise** out of the ground, at the edge of the fog early on and a few tiles away by the end. They walk around buildings, or chew through a fence when that is quicker. They grow tougher each minute.
- **Cover:** shots fly past bushes, fences and signs. Buildings, vehicles and trees stop them.
- **Money:** kills drop money, and money levels you up. Each level-up offers three cards, and pushes nearby zombies back. Some cards are rare and count twice.
- **Health:** some kills drop health instead of money. How often depends on the difficulty.
- **Grenades:** you start with one, or two on Easy. Each recharges in 12 seconds.
- **Barrels:** shooting a red barrel sets it off. Hold X, and your guns go for the barrels in view. A blast close to you hurts you too.
- **Hordes** arrive during the night, each led by a gold-lit leader. The leader drops a chest.
- **Dawn:** the Harvester wakes behind you, and the dead grow 1% stronger every second. Run back to the farmhouse's front step to survive.
- **Falling:** if you fall, you rise as one of the dead and lead them to the next farm.

The pause menu offers Resume, Settings, Photo mode, New farm and Quit to title.

### The two screens

The top screen is the main view, in 2.5D, lit by your lantern and fading into fog. The bottom screen is the map, seen from above.

- The map is bright inside the view's cone and darker outside it.
- A zombie shows on the map only while you look at it. Out of sight, it fades where you last saw it.
- Once the night ends, the map shows the whole horde.

### The end screen

When you fall or get home, the top screen sums up the run. It shows on one screen too.

- **Damage:** each weapon you held, most damage first, with its damage and its damage a second over the time you held it. Grenades and barrels share a row. The last row is the whole run.
- **Build:** the weapons you ended with over your passives, each with its level. A powerup at its top level shows in gold.

### Difficulties

Pick a difficulty under Play with Left and Right, or tap its arrows. A new save starts on Easy, and the game remembers your choice.

| Difficulty | Bites | Health drops | You start with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | Gentle | Often | A pistol, a knife and two grenades |
| Normal | They hurt | Scarce | A pistol, a knife and a grenade |
| Hard | Every bite counts | Rarer still | A pistol, a knife and a grenade |

A player card can change your starting weapons.

## Powerups

Powerups are the weapons and passives on level-up cards. The first level-up takes 2 money, and each one after costs more.

- Each level-up offers three cards, drawn from 17 weapons and 17 passives.
- A run carries at most six weapons and six passives. Once both are full, the cards only level what you carry.
- When nothing remains to level, the cards offer a snack, an ammo crate or pocket change.

Each weapon covers a zone, shown on its card:

| Zone | Where it hits |
|---|---|
| Ahead | The view the top screen shows. The pistol also shoots anything within 70 pixels, whichever way you face. |
| Ahead, then all round | A burst's first shot goes ahead. Each later shot goes to the nearest zombie the burst hasn't hit, on any side. |
| Around | A ring centered on you |
| Behind | Behind you |
| Map | Crowds anywhere on the farm |

### Finds and unlocks

Eight powerups are open from the first night. Each of the rest has a condition: kills, a long enough night, a level, nights played or dawns survived. Kills and nights add up over every run. Night length and level count your best single run.

Meeting a condition makes the powerup findable. A level-up may then offer it on a green FOUND card, and taking that card unlocks it for good.

- A run can find two powerups at most.
- A find appears only when you have a free slot of its kind. Keep a weapon slot open to find new weapons.
- Some finds appear only after a set minute of the night.
- The biggest finds appear only in a leader's chest. A chest gives a free level-up, which holds a find whenever one is waiting.

The title screen counts your unlocked powerups and the finds that wait for you, and names the condition you are closest to. The game-over screen names what the run found.

## Menus and settings

The title's bottom screen lists Play, Map, Settings, Stats and Credits, and Quit where the game can quit. Up and Down choose a row, A or a tap opens it, and B goes back. The top screen shows the game's version.

- **Map** picks a hand-made map, or a random farm, for every run after.
- **Stats** lists your records, including the best damage a second over a whole run. It also counts the cards and mod packs that loaded.
- **History**, from Stats with A or its button, shows the last 10 runs, newest first, five to a page. Each run lists its difficulty, Turned or Home at dawn, its time, level and damage a second, and the powerups it ended with. Left and Right turn the page.
- **Credits** names who made the game, its art, its template and its font.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Top brightness, Bottom brightness | Each screen's backlight. RG DS only. |
| Volume, Rumble | Sound level, and the motor on and off |
| Show FPS | The frame-rate overlay |
| Screens | One or two screens for a run. Not shown on a phone. |
| Touch controls, Grenades | See [Touch](#touch). Shown only where the screen takes touches. |
| Player | Your look, from the player cards, shown walking on the top screen |
| Enemies | Each kind of zombie, walking, with its numbers. A turns a kind on or off. At least one stays on. |
| Game folder | Android only. See [Where files go](#where-files-go). |
| Reset progress | Clears kills, records and found powerups after a second press. Settings stay. |

Settings from the pause menu leave out Player, Enemies, Game folder and Reset progress.

## Screenshots and photo mode

R2 saves both screens, the top above the bottom, as `screenshots/shot-NNNN.png` beside the save. On Android, screenshots go to the game folder's `screenshots/`. The first screenshot asks you to choose a game folder if you have none.

Photo mode, from the pause menu, freezes the run, horde and all, to frame a shot. Its settings change only the picture.

| Action | Pad | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turn the camera around you | Left and Right, or the stick | Left and Right arrows | Stick left and right, or the turn strip |
| Zoom | Up and Down | Up and Down arrows | Stick up and down |
| Raise or lower the camera | Hold L1 with Up and Down | Hold 1 with Up and Down | Hold Height with the stick |
| Choose a setting | L2 | Q | Setting |
| Change it | R2 | 2 | Change |
| Put the cross back where the camera looks, on Place | X | S | Reset |
| Take back the last prop, on Props or Place | Y | A | Change on Take back |
| Take a photo | R1, or tap | W | Tap the picture |
| Back to the pause menu | B or Start | Z, Enter or Esc | Back |

The settings:

- **Time:** Now, Midnight, Small hours, First light, Daybreak or Dawn.
- **Fog:** Clear, Thin, Usual, Thick or Pea soup.
- **Lantern:** Out, Dim, Usual, Bright or Blazing.
- **Pose:** R2 picks a zombie near where the camera looks, and each press picks the next of the eight nearest. Left and Right turn it. Up and Down step it through its walk, hurt, flash and dead frames.
- **Props:** Left and Right choose a prop, such as a barrier, barrel, tree, car, or one of your own. R2 drops it on the cross, facing the camera. Up and Down turn the last one dropped.
- **Place:** the d-pad slides the cross up to 12 tiles away, and R2 drops the chosen prop there.

After each drop, the cross steps aside so props stand in a row. The map shows the cross on Props and Place. Props and poses stand only in the picture, and go when you leave.

A photo leaves out the HUD and the guides. It saves the top screen as `screenshots/photo-NNNN.png` and its middle square as `photo-NNNN-square.png`. In a browser and on muOS, which cannot save files, R1 or a tap hides the guides for your device's own screenshot.

## Platforms

- **Browser:** pick One screen or Two screens at the start. The top-left button fills the screen, where the browser allows it. Sound starts with your first click or key. The save stays in the browser. Screenshots, brightness and rumble do nothing, and neither do Esc and Home.
- **Android:** one screen, locked to landscape, with the system bars hidden. A swipe from the edge shows them for a moment. On a dual-screen handheld, the second screen shows the bottom screen and plays as on the RG DS.
- **muOS:** one screen, no touch. L2 aims grenades. Main menu > Quit, or holding Menu for a second, returns to muOS.
- **Desktop:** one window that plays as on a phone. Menus show both screens side by side, or stacked in a narrow window. The mouse works the touch controls.

## Make your own

Cards change zombies and your look. Props and maps add your own sprites and farms. Mod packs change anything in the game's data. Make them in the card editor, or see the [modding reference](../../docs/modding.md) for every file format.

### Where files go

The game reads your files from its `data/` folder, beside `game_data.pak`, when it starts.

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `data/enemies/` | Enemy cards, `<id>.enemy.png`. Each adds a kind of zombie or replaces one. |
| `data/players/` | Player cards, `<id>.player.png`. Each is a look, and may set your starting weapons. |
| `data/props/` | Props, `<id>.prop.png`: your own sprites for maps |
| `data/maps/` | Maps, `<id>.map.txt`: hand-made farms |
| `data/mods/` | Mod packs, `<name>.pak`, laid over the game's data in name order |

The game skips a file that cannot load, and the title screen shows the first reason. Some apps strip a card's text when they save the PNG again, so share the file itself.

- **Android:** choose a folder with Settings > Game folder, such as a new `Last Harvest` folder in `Documents`. The game makes the folders above in it, plus `screenshots/`. New files load at the next start. Until you choose one, the game reads `Android/data/com.kylejameswalker.lastharvest/files/data/`.
- **Browser:** press Add mod on the start screen and pick a `.pak`. × removes one. On the game's site, your saved card editor work also plays, with no export.

## The card editor

The [card editor](https://kylejameswalker.github.io/last-harvest/editor/) makes enemy cards, player cards, props and maps in a browser, and edits the data files. It keeps your work in the browser until you delete it. It has Undo, but no History.

### Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| New | Starts a card from a plain figure. Start the name with `PLAYER_` for a player card, `PROP_` for a prop, or `MAP_` for a map. |
| Import | Opens `.enemy.png`, `.player.png`, `.prop.png` and `.map.txt` files, and everything in a `.pak`. It names the saved cards it replaced. |
| Export | Downloads the open card's or prop's PNG. On a map, downloads `<id>.pak` with the map and every prop it places. |
| Export all | Downloads everything as one mod pack, `my-cards.pak`, for the game's `data/mods`. |
| Sound | Gives an enemy card its own death sound: an Ogg, WAV or MP3, kept as an Ogg of up to 512 KB |
| Delete card | Removes the open card. Press it twice. |
| Game data | Picks your own `game_data.pak`. See [Game data](#game-data). |
| Help | A short guide to making a card |

### Make a card

- Press **New** and type the card's id: letters, digits and `_`.
- Draw each part from the list: walking front, side and back, then the flash, hurt and dead frames. An enemy's walk takes 1 to 8 frames. Leave flash and hurt clear for the game to draw them.
- Press **T** for the Stats page, and set the name and numbers.
- Press **Save**, then **Export**.
- Copy the PNG into the game's `data/enemies` or `data/players` folder and start the game. Settings > Enemies or Settings > Player shows it.

Other tools:

- **Compare** shows the parts side by side.
- **Hurt** and **Kill** play a hit or a death on the figure in the previews.
- **Detail** - and + redraw a card or prop 2x finer or coarser at the same size in the game. Use it sparingly, since the rest of the game uses plain pixels.
- **Alpha** - and + make a prop's frame on show more or less see-through. Shift changes every frame.
- **Grenade** (W), on a player card's walking parts, places where you wear a ready grenade. **Hide** leaves it off that facing.

The palette is PICO-8's 32 colors. **Palette file** loads a `.hex` palette, such as one downloaded from [Lospec](https://lospec.com/palette-list).

### The Stats page

Rename and About set the name and the one-sentence about line. For an enemy card, - and + step each number, ten times as far with Shift:

- Health, Speed, Bite, Money, Weight and Gibs.
- Arrives after (minutes), Chance and Chance per minute.
- Charge: its speed near you, as a multiple.
- Spits from (pixels) and Burrows after (seconds). 0 turns either off.
- Heavy, Leads hordes and Harvester toggles.
- Blood: < and > step its blood color through the palette.
- Drop own: removes the card's own death sound.

A chart under the numbers shows how a night's spawns split between the game's kinds and your cards, minute by minute. Its legend flags any kind that never comes.

For a player card, the Stats page sets the name, the about line, and the weapons each difficulty starts with. Default keeps the game's own.

### Props and maps

Props list under Props as `PROP_<ID>`. The stand, fps, Cover, Broken frame and HP buttons set how the prop stands and breaks.

Maps list under Maps as `MAP_<ID>`. The canvas shows the map from above, and a line under it says whether the game would load it.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ground | Paints a ground code. A road paints three lanes wide. Shift-click paints a straight line. |
| Prop | Places a sprite from the picker: the game's own, then yours |
| Move | Drags a prop or fence and shows its hit points and cover for this map. Defaults restores its own. |
| Erase | Takes a piece off |
| Fence | Drags a fence, with its gate mid-bottom |
| Spawn, Home | Set your start tile and the tile you run home to |
| Width, Height | Grow or shrink the map 4 tiles at a time, or 16 with Shift |
| Randomize | Lays out a random farm, every piece a prop to edit |

Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or the arrows move the view.

### The Data pages

Each data file lists under Data, and each section is a page, such as Data > tuning > player. A page shows one line a row, with its comment under it.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| - and + | Step a number |
| < and > | Step a color through the palette |
| Edit | Type any value |
| x, Add line | Remove a line, or add one |
| Up, Down | Page through a long section |
| Revert | Go back to the last save |
| Save | Keep the file, only when the game would read it |

A weapon in `powerups.txt` opens as a weapon page:

- **Does** picks its behavior.
- Each stat shows its value at every level. Pick a level, then - and + step that level's value.
- < and > step its effect, bullet and sound. **Own** goes back to the behavior's own.
- A panel plays the effect and bullet. **Style** opens the bullet's shot style, where **Paint** opens a sprite to draw.
- **Lines** shows the raw lines, and **Weapon** goes back.
- **Try it** opens the weapon range in a new tab, with unsaved changes. Allow pop-ups if the browser blocks it.

Export all carries changed data files into the mod pack.

### The weapon range

The range tests one weapon at its level against a ring of eight zombies that rise again as they die. The ring never bites, and no level-ups come. The top screen shows the damage per second over the last five seconds.

| Action | Buttons | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Step the level | Up and Down | Level - and Level + |
| Pick the zombie kind, cards included | Left and Right | < Kind and Kind > |
| Switch between firing by hand and on cooldown | Select | By hand / On cooldown |
| Fire by hand | X | Fire |

Restart, in the pause menu, starts the range afresh.

### Game data

On the game's site, the editor previews your card on the farm. Served without game data, it shows a plain floor. **Game data** picks your own `game_data.pak`, which wins over the site's. The editor keeps it in the browser, never sends it anywhere, and refuses a pack from another version of the game.

### Offline, iPad and phone

The editor keeps an offline copy once opened, and loads a new release first when it can. Add it to an iPad's home screen for the whole screen.

- Draw with the Apple Pencil or a finger. Pinch to zoom the canvas, and press Fit to reset it.
- Drag the 2.5D view to turn it, and pinch it to move closer.
- The toolbar under the panes stands in for the keyboard. Its Shift stays on until you tap it again.
- A Shift pad in a bottom corner lights until your next pencil press uses it.
- On a phone, the previews sit under the editing pane. Drag two fingers off the canvas to scroll.
- A copy on the iOS home screen keeps its own storage, apart from Safari's.
