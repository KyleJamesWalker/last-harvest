LastHarvest

Survive the night on a farm overrun by zombies. LastHarvest is made for the ANBERNIC RG DS Plus, a handheld with two screens: a 3D view on the top one and the map on the touchscreen below.

PlayBoth screens in your browser, with a keyboard or a gamepad. A click on the lower screen is a touch. Card editorDraw your own zombies and player looks as cards for the game.

Source, releases and how to install it on the handheld: github.com/KyleJamesWalker/last-harvest
