Last Harvest

Survive the night! Your farm is overrun by zombies. Last Harvest is a survivors-like made for dual-screen handhelds, and this web build lets you try it out before installing it on the device.

PlayBoth screens in your browser, with a keyboard or a gamepad. A click on the lower screen throws a grenade. Card editorCreate your own maps, players, props and enemies.

Source, releases and how to install it on the handheld: github.com/KyleJamesWalker/last-harvest
