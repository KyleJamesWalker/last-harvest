How it was run

By default the scripted player walks a circle (or a figure eight), turns round every 6 s, lobs a grenade ahead every 12 s and never picks a level-up, so a forced build stays fixed; run.py --player changes that, and each tier's settings are listed below. It never dies; its first fall is recorded, and survival is the mean time to it with a night that never fell counted as its whole length. Pressure runs lay tools/matrix/pressure over the data: spawns from 2/s rising 2.5/s per minute up to 25/s, hordes of 40 + 8 per minute, and a zombie cap of 600, so strong builds stop hitting the same ceiling. The random-build fit is a ridge regression on level fractions with every weapon pair and weapon–passive pair as interaction terms; R² is shown beside it.
