Making a card

A card is one PNG that holds a figure's art and its numbers. Enemy cards add a kind of zombie to the night, or replace one of the game's own; player cards change how you look and what you start with.

  1. New starts a card from a plain figure. Type its id: letters, digits and _. Start the name with PLAYER_ for a player card.
  2. Draw its five parts from the list: walking front, side and back, then a flash and a hurt frame for each facing. An enemy's walk can have 1 to 8 frames. Compare shows all five side by side.
  3. Stats (T) sets its name and numbers. For an enemy, the chart shows how often it comes through the night beside the game's own kinds; for a player, which weapons each difficulty starts with.
  4. Save, then Export downloads id.enemy.png or id.player.png.
  5. Copy it into the game's data/enemies or data/players folder, beside game_data.pak, and start the game. Settings > Enemies and Settings > Player show it. Export all instead downloads every card as one my-cards.pak for the game's data/mods folder.

Your cards stay in this browser until you delete them. Import opens a card someone shared with you, or every card in a .pak. Game data lets you pick your own game_data.pak so the previews show the farm; it never leaves your browser.

Some apps strip a PNG's text when they re-save it, which takes a card's numbers with it: share the file itself.
