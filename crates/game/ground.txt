# The ground a map paints, one code a cell. Each [c] section is a code, one character, and the map editor's
# paint buttons follow this order. Its lines:
#   name Text           the paint button's label
#   art NAME            the tile, from art.txt; with several frames, cells pick between them by where they are
#   joins across|down   a run's cells take the first, middle and last frames of art, left to right or top to bottom
#   cell open|road|path|field   what the cell is: open ground, a road, a path that joins the paths and roads
#                       beside it, picking art's frame by which sides join, or a field the farm grows on
#   crop TOP MIDDLE BOTTOM   a crop standing on it, from art.txt: TOP at the north end of a run, BOTTOM at the
#                       south end, MIDDLE between; TOP picks between its frames as art does
# The first code is what a map's new ground is.
