# What each effect, swing and animal plays: a sprite from art.txt or a player's prop (PROP_<ID>), and how fast.
#   art NAME      the sprite; its frames play in order
#   fps N         frames a second
#   life N        a swing's seconds
#   reach N       how far out a swing sweeps, as a fraction of the weapon's range or area
#   alpha N       how opaque it draws, 0.1 to 1; 1 when left out
#   fade_in N     the fraction of its play, 0 to 1, it takes to come in from clear
#   fade_out N    the fraction of its play, 0 to 1, it takes to fade away at the end
# Alpha and fades play on effects that play once where they happen, such as blasts and puffs, and on swings.
#   color RRGGBB  a shot style: its tracer's and sparks' color, as palette.txt writes one
#   length N      a shot's tracer, in pixels
#   size N        the tracer's width at its head, and about the sparks', in pixels
#   sparks N      sparks a gun throws forward from its muzzle with each shot
#   impact N      sparks where each bullet hits a zombie, a prop or the ground
#   core N        the white-hot flare out of the muzzle, in pixels
#   tracer NAME   the sprite a shot's tracer stretches along its path, its head to the right
#   spark NAME    the sprite of each spark, its frames played over the spark's life
#   smoke NAME    the sprite of each smoke puff, likewise
#   flare NAME    the sprite of the muzzle flare, pointing right, its frames played over the flare's life
# A shot without a sprite named draws a plain colored shape in its place. A sprite can be a player's prop.
# A section that sets any shot style flies as a tracer when a weapon fires it as its bullets, and needs no art;
# what it leaves out comes from [bullet], which a gun without a `bullet` line fires.
# A sprite standing as a slab lies flat and turns with the swing; anything else faces the camera.
# A section with a name of your own is an effect a weapon in powerups.txt can play with `effect NAME` or
# fire as its bullets with `bullet NAME`.
