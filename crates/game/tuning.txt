# The numbers the game plays by that no card, prop or powerup sets. Each [section] holds `key number` lines;
# a mod's tuning.txt only needs the lines it changes. Distances are in pixels (a tile is 16), times in seconds,
# angles in radians. Any other [section] is a difficulty: name, blurb, kit (easy, normal or hard: whose starting
# weapons and grenades), damage, hurt_time, health_drop and heal, and any section.key it changes, such as spawn.rate.
# What it leaves out comes from its kit's difficulty and the sections above. The title lists them in this order.
